About

Systems for the full lifecycle of AI

My work spans the stack that turns a model into a product. On the training side, I build distributed-training infrastructure and dashboards — DeepSpeed and FSDP pipelines, multi-GPU comparisons, and parallel post-training sweeps with crash recovery. On the agent side, I design multi-agent orchestration with LangGraph and message-bus architectures, plus self-learning training loops.

For retrieval, I build production RAG with hybrid search, reranking, and full citation traceability. And because none of it ships without observability, I build tracers and judge panels that make agent behavior searchable and debuggable. I also write — turning hard ML systems concepts into interactive, teachable handbooks.
