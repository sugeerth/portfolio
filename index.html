About

What I work on

Across these projects I keep coming back to a few areas. On the training side — distributed-training setups and dashboards: DeepSpeed and FSDP pipelines, multi-GPU comparisons, and post-training sweeps with crash recovery. On the agent side — multi-agent orchestration with LangGraph and message-bus patterns, and self-learning training loops with hierarchical judges.

For retrieval — RAG with hybrid search, cross-encoder reranking, and citation traceability. And since these systems are hard to debug, I build observability tracers and judge panels that make agent behavior easier to follow. I also enjoy writing — turning ML systems concepts into interactive explainers.
